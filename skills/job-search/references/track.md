# Track

Statuses: submitted, confirmed, interview, offer, rejected, withdrawn, closed. Record changes with `J ops` transition. After 14 days a submitted job comes back as `follow_up_due`: ask the person what happened and record signals (`J ops` signal: reply, interview, rejection, offer). Never contact an employer for the person. Skips carry a reason: too_senior, too_junior, place, pay, company, stack, other.
