# Contacts (on request, Haiku subagent)

Ask how many jobs; 5 if the person does not say. For each, find the hiring manager, team lead or founder through Tenjin's `request` tool, stating the need ("people at <company> with title <x>"); name no seller. Every contact needs a source link: `J ops` `contact` refuses one without. If none is found, say so. Messages are drafts: `J write-prompt --contacts <key,key>` gives an Opus call; the person edits and sends them.
